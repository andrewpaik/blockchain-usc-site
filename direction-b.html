What this club is

Two desks, one application

Research & Investments

Research on the digital-asset ecosystem for bounties, publications, and competitions. The desk manages a portfolio of more than $100,000 in digital assets; every investment is pitched and voted on by members before a dollar moves.

Projects

Grants, hackathons, and consulting clients, for real prize money and funding. Marketing, events, and partnership work runs on the same desk.

What members get

An education program that assumes zero background. Professional research platforms and premium AI tooling. Sponsored travel to conferences like ETHDenver and UBC, and an annual conference co-hosted with VanEck alongside Circle and Solana.

Where alumni land: BlackRock, Coinbase, Jump Trading, Polychain Capital, 50T.
