The operation

/about

Blockchain@USC runs as two desks. The research and investments desk writes on the digital-asset ecosystem for bounties, publications, and competitions, and manages a portfolio of more than $100,000: every position is pitched and voted on by members.

The projects desk builds for grants, hackathons, and consulting clients, and takes home real prize money and funding. Marketing, events, and partnership work runs alongside it.

Our alumni have recently landed at BlackRock, Coinbase, Jump Trading, Polychain Capital, and 50T, and founders in the club have raised more than $7M from investors including Paradigm.

Membership includes

  • Education program assuming zero background
  • Professional research platforms, premium AI tooling
  • Sponsored travel: ETHDenver, UBC, and more
  • A vote on every portfolio position
  • Annual conference with VanEck, Circle, Solana
  • Every major welcome

The book

/investments

Dorm Capital is our seat in DormDAO: a student-run investment DAO uniting 17 university blockchain clubs across the US, Canada, and the UK. Each school manages its own on-chain portfolio and competes on a transparent, real-time leaderboard.

The numbers stay public, in ETH and in USD, wins and losses alike. Real capital and real accountability, before graduation.
